# Project Reports

This directory contains documented findings from the Strait of Hormuz Supply Chain Analytics project.

## Planned Reports

1. `data_quality_report.md` — Documents data quality checks, confirmed issues, and limitations.
2. `maritime_traffic_analysis.md` — Presents findings about vessel traffic and transit patterns.
3. `petroleum_flow_analysis.md` — Documents findings from available petroleum transportation data.
4. `supply_chain_risk_assessment.md` — Evaluates observed supply chain risks and potential operational implications.

Reports will be written after the relevant analyses are completed. Findings and recommendations will be supported by evidence from the selected datasets.

No analytical results will be assumed before the data has been examined.
